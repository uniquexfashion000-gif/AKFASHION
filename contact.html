Frequently Asked Questions

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day return policy on all unworn watches. Watches must be in original condition with all documentation. Simply contact us via WhatsApp or email to initiate a return.

How long does shipping take?

Shipping within Pakistan takes 3-5 business days. International orders take 7-14 business days depending on the destination. Tracking information is provided with every order.

Are your watches authenticated?

Yes, every AKFASHION watch comes with a certificate of authenticity. We guarantee 100% authentic, premium quality timepieces with Swiss movements and the finest materials.

What warranty coverage do I get?

All AKFASHION watches include a 5-year international warranty covering mechanical defects. Damage from misuse or accidents is not covered. Warranty cards are included with your purchase.

Can I customize my watch?

We offer limited customization options on select models. Contact our concierge team via WhatsApp or email to discuss personalization options for your timepiece.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes, we accept multiple payment options including installment plans for purchases over a certain amount. Discuss available options with our team when placing your order.
